We're Freedom Firea fire safety business that hires the people the job market walks past, and trains every one of them to a certified trade.

The Open Door is not a scheme. It is how the company is crewed: T Level placements, college placements, Job Centre referrals, and adjustments that make a job fit a person. Paid from day one, tickets funded, mentored on the vans until they can sign their own work. Nine people came in this way. This site is theirs, and the evidence behind it.

Meet the nine →

Over five years, nine people came in through four doors: a T Level placement, a college placement, the Job Centre, and study alongside work. Every one of them is a route the award names.
