01 — Who we are

A fire safety company that shows its working

Freedom Fire & Safety Ltd was founded in Bury in August 2021 and now protects homes and businesses across the United Kingdom. We supply, fit, service and certify the equipment that fire safety law requires — and we keep the records that prove it was done properly.

02 — The work

What we fit, test and certify

Four disciplines, one chain of responsibility. Miss a link and the whole thing fails when it matters.

Extinguishers

Supply, installation, annual servicing and maintenance — the right type in the right place, and a certificate at every visit.

Alarm systems

Designed, installed, commissioned and maintained, so a building warns the people inside it long before anyone smells smoke.

Emergency lighting

Installation, periodic testing and certification — the difference between an orderly exit and a stairwell in the dark.

Assessment & training

Fire risk assessments in line with the Fire Safety Order, plus warden, marshal and extinguisher training for the people on site.

05 — Freedom Freight

We watch our own supply chain

Most firms our size order from a wholesaler and hope. We went upstream — vetting suppliers ourselves, working with production partners overseas, and building our own system to follow every order from the factory floor to the warehouse in Bury.

06 — The code

Four things we don't bend on

01

Certified, or not at all

If it cannot be certified and documented, it does not get signed off. The paperwork is the product as much as the hardware is.

02

Say the real date

Because we track our own stock from origin, we can give a delivery date we actually believe — and say so plainly when it slips.

03

Explain it properly

Most clients did not choose to become the responsible person. Part of the job is making the duty understandable rather than frightening.

04

Train our own
