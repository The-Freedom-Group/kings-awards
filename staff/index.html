Why this company can be judged at all

Fire safety is the main focus.

The award bars any organisation whose main focus is social mobility. Ours is fire safety, done properly and certified. The open door is how we crew that business — a way of hiring and training, not what the company is for.

Not a training provider.

Training providers and educational organisations may only apply with a business, or for an initiative for their own employees. Freedom Fire is a fire safety business that trains its own people, so neither restriction applies.

Where you start doesn't decide where you finish

We're a fire safety business first — the open door is how we crew it. No degree required, no connections needed: paid work, funded tickets, and a certified trade for people the job market usually passes over.

It is how we hire, not a side project. The crew in Part 03 is this door's output — which is why it cannot be the company's "main focus": the main focus is fire safety, done properly.

The two-year test

The award requires the programme to have run for at least two years by the time it is assessed. Applications for the 2027 awards close on 8 September 2026, so the earliest dated record — first apprentice's contract, first funded course invoice, first placement letter — has to be from 8 September 2024 or before.

If it is younger than that, this category must wait a cycle. Say so now, not in the application.

07The code

Four things we don't bend on

§1

Certified, or not at all

If it cannot be certified and documented, it does not get signed off. The paperwork is the product as much as the hardware is.

§2

Say the real date

Because we track our own stock from origin, we can give a delivery date we actually believe — and say so plainly when it slips.

§3

Explain it properly

Most clients did not choose to become the responsible person. Part of the job is making the duty understandable rather than frightening.

§4

Keep the door open

Hire on attitude, fund the tickets, mentor on the vans — so the trade has engineers in ten years' time. Part 04 is how.
