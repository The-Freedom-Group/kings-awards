The exclusions, answered

Fire safety is the main focus.

The award bars any organisation whose main focus is social mobility. Ours is fire safety, done properly and certified. The open door is a way of hiring and training, not what the company is for.

Not a training provider.

Training providers may only apply with a business, or for their own employees. Freedom Fire is a fire safety business that trains its own people, so neither restriction applies.

The two-year test

The award requires the programme to have run for at least two years. Applications for the 2027 awards close on 8 September 2026, so the earliest dated record — first placement letter, first funded course invoice, first apprentice's contract — has to be from 8 September 2024 or before.

If it is younger than that, this category waits a cycle. Say so now, not in the application.

05What we are — the standing commitments

Not a scheme. A standing commitment.

Two things the company signed up to that sit behind the open door — each a public commitment with a body behind it, and each needing its paperwork in the application.

T Level Ambassadors Employer ambassador · T Level Ambassador Network

Freedom Fire hosts T Level industry placements and speaks for them to other employers. Four of the nine people in Part 04 came through one. This is the award's activity (a) — work experience — as a standing arrangement, not a one-off.

Evidence to supplyThe ambassador listing or confirmation email, the date the company joined, the placements hosted per year, and the colleges they came from.
Disability Confident Employer Disability Confident scheme · Department for Work and Pensions

A public commitment to recruit, retain and adjust for disabled people. It is why the recruitment process — activity (d) — is open to everyone in practice, and why Jojo's story in Part 04 is possible. The level held and the certificate go in the application.

Evidence to supplyThe scheme level (Committed, Employer or Leader), the certificate and its date, and the adjustments policy in writing. Bill's note says "DSA employer" — confirm it is the Disability Confident scheme.

07The code

Four things we don't bend on

§1

Certified, or not at all

If it cannot be certified and documented, it does not get signed off. The paperwork is the product as much as the hardware is.

§2

Say the real date

Because we track our own stock from origin, we can give a delivery date we actually believe — and say so plainly when it slips.

§3

Explain it properly

Most clients did not choose to become the responsible person. Part of the job is making the duty understandable rather than frightening.

§4

Keep the door open

Hire on attitude, fund the tickets, mentor on the vans — so the trade has engineers in ten years' time. Part 03 is how.
