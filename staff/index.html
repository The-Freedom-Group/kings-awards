04 — What we are

Not a scheme. A standing commitment.

Two things the company signed up to that sit behind the door — each a public commitment with a body behind it, and each needing its paperwork in the application.

T Level Ambassador Network · employer ambassador

T Level Ambassadors

Freedom Fire hosts T Level industry placements and speaks for them to other employers. Four of the nine people in Part 03 came through one. This is the award's activity (a), work experience, as a standing arrangement — not a one-off.

Evidence to supplyThe ambassador listing or confirmation email, the date the company joined, placements hosted per year, and the colleges they came from.
Disability Confident scheme · Department for Work and Pensions

Disability Confident Employer

A public commitment to recruit, retain and adjust for disabled people. It is why the recruitment process — activity (d) — is open to everyone in practice, and why Jojo's story in Part 03 is possible. The level held and the certificate go in the application.

Evidence to supplyThe scheme level (Committed, Employer or Leader), the certificate and its date, and the adjustments policy in writing. Bill's note says "DSA employer" — confirm it is the Disability Confident scheme.
05 — The two-year test, and the proofs

Settle this first.

The award requires the programme to have run for at least two years. Applications for the 2027 awards close on 8 September 2026, so the earliest dated record has to be from 8 September 2024 or before. If it is younger than that, this category waits a cycle.

The two-year test

The earliest dated record — first placement letter, first funded course invoice, first apprentice's contract — is what the assessors will ask for. Find it before writing another word of the application.
