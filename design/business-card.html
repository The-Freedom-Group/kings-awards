Print notes. At this scale 10 px is 1 mm, so the contact lines are about 7 pt and the name about 30 pt, which is standard card sizing. Black is the site's carbon (#101010) rather than pure black, so a matte or soft-touch laminate keeps the two blacks close; the pink is #FF2D8D (Pantone 806 C is the nearest spot colour) and reads best as a spot ink or a raised spot-UV line; the porcelain type is #F4F1EB. The pink thread is the site's line; the small dot is where it stops.
