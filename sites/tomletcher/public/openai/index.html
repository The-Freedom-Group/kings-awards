built by a young team in Manchester.

Freedom Fire & Safety sells extinguishers, blankets, signs and safety kit across the UK, on eBay, Amazon, Temu, TikTok Shop and our own store. We build our own software rather than buying more of it: one system that runs the warehouse, answers questions about the business, and puts what needs attention in front of the right person.

01 · The centrepiece

Freedom Command Centre

One screen for the whole operation. It reads our Linnworks feeds (open orders every five minutes, stock hourly, sales daily), runs the rest of the day forward minute by minute, and shows each person what matters now. Three workflows, built to work properly rather than twenty half-built ones.

Founder Dashboard

What needs me today?

Priorities, approvals and blockers on one page, most urgent first. Every decision arrives as a prepared case with the numbers behind it.

  • Each carrier's van forecast to the minute
  • Purchases, prices and refunds: approve or decline in one tap

02 · STAFF COMMAND VIEW

Staff Command View

Who is doing what?

Every person's day plan, workday sheet, orders packed and points. When a collection is at risk it works out who to move, and the forecast updates the moment you move them.

  • Live day plan for the whole floor
  • Sheets, late jobs and photo proof to sign off
  • A suggested fix, tried against the whole day first

03 · AI / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Ask the Business

Questions in, answers out.

Plain-English questions about orders, stock, products, channels and the team, answered from the data with the working and the sources shown. In the demo a rule-based engine answers from sample data; in the live Hub our assistant uses a language model on the real records.

02 · Built and live

What we've built

Everything here was designed and built in-house, most of it by people who joined us through placements and T Levels.

Live

Fire Cover Calculator

Tell it the premises, how big it is, how many storeys, and what could burn there. It applies the British Standard for extinguisher cover (BS 5306-8), picks the right type and number for every floor, shows its working, and prices the kit.

  • Quick in about two minutes, or full depth with a floor-area map tool
  • Every quantity explained, not just stated
  • A priced, printable specification at the end
Open the live tool
Live

Freedom Freight

Our own tracker for the containers we ship from China. Every order is followed from the factory, through the Chinese port, across the water and through UK customs to our warehouse in Bury, about 100 days door to door.

  • Every container, its stage and its days, on one journey
  • Feeds stock cover in the Command Centre: what lands before what runs out
  • A public version for partners; costs stay internal
