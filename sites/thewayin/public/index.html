Part 02 / The programme
02The programme

Four signs. Turn each one over.

The award names four activities. These are the four, as we do them — each with the evidence it needs behind it. It is how we hire, not a side project: the people in Part 04 are this programme's output.

Floor planThe route through the door: from first day to training the next person
Stage 1

Day one

A paid job from the first day, whichever way you came in. No unpaid trial, no "see how it goes". TK — the starting wage and hours

Stage 2

First tickets

The first qualifications you need to work safely, booked and paid for by the company in your first weeks. TK — which qualifications, and their cost

Stage 3

On the vans

Out on real jobs alongside an experienced engineer, who shows you the work and checks yours, until you can do it unsupervised.

Stage 4

Certified

Qualified in your own right and signing off your own work. TK — how long that typically takes

Stage 5

The mentor

Now the experienced engineer in the van, training the next person who came in through the same door.

Part 03 / The two-year test
03The two-year test

Inspection tag. Settle this first.

The award requires the programme to have run for at least two years. Applications for the 2027 awards close on 8 September 2026, so the earliest dated record has to be from 8 September 2024 or before. If it is younger than that, this category waits a cycle.

The two-year test

The earliest dated record — first placement letter, first funded course invoice, first apprentice's contract — is what the assessors will ask for. Find it before writing another word of the application.

Say so now, not in the application, if it is younger than two years.

Part 06 / What we are
06What we are, and what we are not

Two plates. Two exclusions.

Two public commitments that sit behind the way in, each with a body behind it and each needing its paperwork in the application. Then the two things the award says an applicant must not be, and why neither applies.

Seal
TKT Level Ambassador Network · employer ambassador

T Level Ambassadors

Freedom Fire hosts T Level industry placements and speaks for them to other employers. Four of the nine people in Part 04 came through one. This is the award's activity (a), work experience, as a standing arrangement — not a one-off.

Evidence to supplyThe ambassador listing or confirmation email, the date the company joined, placements hosted per year, and the colleges they came from.
Disability Confident · Committed · Level 1Level 2, Employer, in progress
Disability Confident scheme · Department for Work and Pensions

Disability Confident

A public commitment to recruit, retain and adjust for disabled people. It is why the recruitment process — activity (d) — is open to everyone in practice. Level 1, Committed, is held; Level 2, Employer, is the next step and is not claimed until awarded.

Evidence to supplyThe Level 1 confirmation and its date, the Committed badge artwork from the confirmation pack, and the adjustments policy in writing.
✕

Not our main focus

The award excludes any organisation whose main focus is social mobility. Ours is fire safety: supply, installation, servicing, assessment and certification, from Bury, nationwide. The way in is how the business is crewed, not what it is for. Does not apply

✕

Not a training provider

Training providers and educational organisations may only apply with a business, or for an initiative for their own employees. Freedom Fire is a fire safety business that trains its own people. Does not apply
