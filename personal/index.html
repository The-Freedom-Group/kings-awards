01 — Origin

It started with one key and one industrial unit.

one.

Bury, Greater Manchester. August 2021. One director, one address, and a sector that does not hand anything to newcomers.

Why trust is the product

Fire safety is a legal duty, not a discretionary purchase. Under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the Fire Safety Act 2021, a named responsible person must hold a suitable and sufficient fire risk assessment for any non-domestic premises.

Which makes trust the product — and makes it very hard to start from nothing.

The register records it plainly: Freedom Fire & Safety Ltd, company number 13589467, incorporated 27 August 2021 with £100 of stated capital, one director, registered in Bury. Tom Letcher was twenty-five.

The business sits in an unglamorous corner of the economy where the consequences of cutting corners are measured in lives: extinguishers that have not been serviced, alarm panels nobody has tested, risk assessments written to be filed rather than acted upon. That is the proposition a fire-safety company lives or dies by — do the work properly, and keep the records that prove it.

The company's own paperwork, at least, has been kept that way. Accounts filed for every year since incorporation. A confirmation statement every year. One name on every filing.

Source: Companies House, company 13589467 — filing history, checked 3 September 2026.

Equipment

Fire extinguishers and emergency lighting — supply, installation, servicing and annual maintenance.

Detection

Fire alarm systems designed, installed, commissioned and maintained.

Assurance

Fire risk assessments in line with the Fire Safety Order, plus fire warden and extinguisher training.

Rather than resell from a wholesaler and compete on price, the company says it went upstream: choosing its own suppliers, specifying its own products, and tracking each consignment from the factory to its warehouse in Bury. Its own account of the wider group describes brands it owns or is developing across fire safety, aerosols, batteries, protective equipment, cleaning and automotive.

The service list and operating model are as described by the company (freedomgroup.uk, checked 3 September 2026). Their verification status is set out in the record.
