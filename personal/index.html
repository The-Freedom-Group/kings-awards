01 — One Unit

It started with one key and one industrial unit.

one.

Bury, Greater Manchester. August 2021. One director, one address, and a sector that does not hand anything to newcomers.

Fire safety is a legal duty, not a discretionary purchase. Under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the Fire Safety Act 2021, a named responsible person must hold a suitable and sufficient fire risk assessment for any non-domestic premises.

Which makes trust the product — and makes it very hard to start from nothing.

The register records it plainly: Freedom Fire & Safety Ltd, company number 13589467, incorporated 27 August 2021, sole director, registered to Unit 19 on the Pilsworth Industrial Estate in Bury. Tom Letcher was twenty-five.

The business sits in an unglamorous corner of the economy where the consequences of cutting corners are measured in lives. Extinguishers that have not been serviced. Alarm panels nobody has tested. Risk assessments written to be filed rather than acted upon. It was built on the opposite proposition: certified work, documented properly, that stands up when an inspector or an insurer asks.

Tom to supply — the genuine beginnings The chapter needs your detail, not a summary. The first premises and what it actually looked like. The first stock you bought and what it cost. The first order and who it was for. What you were doing before August 2021, and why you started rather than stayed employed.

Rather than resell from a wholesaler and compete on price, the company went upstream: vetting its own suppliers, specifying its own products, and tracking every consignment from factory floor to the UK warehouse through a system built in-house. That vertical reach is unusual for an independent firm of this size.

Equipment

Extinguishers and emergency lighting — supply, installation, servicing and annual maintenance, certified at every visit.

Detection

Fire alarm systems designed, installed, commissioned and maintained, so a building warns people before anyone smells smoke.

Assurance

Fire risk assessments in line with the Fire Safety Order, plus warden and extinguisher training, and the records that evidence both.

Clients range from landlords and property managers to schools, care homes, offices, retail, warehousing and manufacturing — and, unusually for the sector, private homeowners as well.

05 — How I Build

The operating philosophy, in his own words.

This page supports a Young Founder application. The award asks for evidence of impact across five themes — fresh ideas and creative thinking, driving growth and industry influence, leading and big-picture thinking, strategic resilience, and customer value. They sit underneath this chapter rather than on top of it: the page should read as a story, not an application form.

The category's three eligibility gates, and the evidence each theme needs, are set out in the Verified Record — with the one unresolved question flagged rather than hidden.

Tom to supply — the whole chapter, one piece per theme Five short pieces, three or four sentences each, in the award's own order. Your voice, not a copywriter's. Beside each is the evidence the assessors will ask for.

1 · Fresh ideas and creative thinking. Owning brands rather than reselling is one real answer; tracking your own supply chain end to end is another. What else does the trade not do? Evidence: a short video, up to two minutes, telling the journey from idea to impact.

2 · Driving growth and industry influence. What changed in your corner of the market because you were in it. Evidence: business performance over the past one to two years showing growth, plus external recognition — speaking invitations with testimonials, awards, or media coverage.

3 · Leading and big-picture thinking. Why the group structure exists, and what the next five years actually require you to build. Evidence: the organisation's long-term vision and how it is being put into practice.

4 · Strategic resilience. A genuine setback and what you did about it. Assessors distrust a founder who cannot name one. Evidence: the challenges faced and how the organisation adapted.

5 · Customer value. What a customer gets from Freedom Fire that they would not get elsewhere. Evidence: how customer satisfaction is measured, and how that measurement has changed a product or a service.

The business

Freedom Fire & Safety Ltd supplies, installs, services and maintains the equipment and documentation that keep a building compliant and its occupants safe. It trades nationwide from its Bury base, serving both businesses and private homeowners.

Equipment

Fire extinguishers and emergency lighting — supply, installation, servicing and annual maintenance, with certification at every visit.

Detection

Fire alarm systems designed, installed, commissioned and maintained.

Assurance

Fire risk assessments in line with the Fire Safety Order, plus fire warden and extinguisher training, and the records that evidence both.

Beyond the core services the company has moved upstream into its own supply chain — sourcing from more than thirty vetted suppliers, working with production partners overseas, and tracking every order from factory to the UK warehouse through a system built in-house.
